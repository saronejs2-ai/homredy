Our story

We exist because renters deserve better information.

Homredy was built out of a simple frustration: why do tenants have to rely on 30-minute site visits and gut feeling to make one of the biggest decisions of their year? Real tenant reviews for smarter rental decisions — that's what we're here to enable.

The problem

The rental market is opaque by default.

Landlords have years of experience navigating lease agreements, deposit deductions, and tenant turnover. First-time renters — and even experienced ones — often have only a short site visit, photos on a listing portal, and an agent's word to go on.

What they can't easily discover: Is the water supply reliable? Does the owner respond to maintenance requests? Were previous tenants' deposits returned fairly? Was the apartment actually in the condition shown?

This information exists — but it's locked inside the experiences of the people who came before you.

Our solution

A community built on honesty.

Homredy unlocks that information. We are a platform where tenants who have lived in a property can share their experience — and where future tenants can read it before making a decision.

Think of it as Glassdoor for rental properties. Honest. Community-powered. Moderated for quality. And entirely free.

We do not list properties for rent. We do not help landlords find tenants. We do not take commissions. Homredy exists only to serve renters.

Community standards

Review Guidelines

Our standards for honest, helpful, and fair tenant reviews.

Homredy is a community-powered platform. The value of every review depends on its honesty, accuracy, and fairness. These guidelines exist to maintain trust for everyone who uses Homredy — including you.

All reviews are manually moderated before publication. Homredy reserves the right to remove, edit, or decline to publish any review that violates these guidelines.

What makes a good review

  • ✅

    Based on personal experience

    Reviews must reflect your direct experience as a tenant at that property. Do not submit reviews on behalf of others, for properties you have not rented, or based on second-hand accounts.

  • ✅

    Factual and accurate

    Only include claims you know to be true from your own experience. Avoid exaggeration. If you are unsure of a fact, leave it out rather than risk inaccuracy.

  • ✅

    Specific and useful

    Helpful reviews describe specific events, timelines, and situations. "The landlord took 3 months to fix the water heater" is more useful than "the landlord was bad." Specificity builds trust.

  • ✅

    Balanced and fair

    Share both positives and negatives where relevant. Even a difficult rental experience may have had aspects that were acceptable. Honest, balanced reviews are the most credible.

What is not allowed

  • 🚫

    Abusive or threatening language

    Reviews must not contain insults, threats, intimidation, or aggressive language directed at any individual or business. Express your experience in civil, measured terms.

  • 🚫

    Discriminatory content

    Reviews must not contain content that discriminates based on religion, caste, race, gender, sexual orientation, disability, or any other protected characteristic.

  • 🚫

    False allegations

    Do not make claims that you cannot substantiate from your own direct experience. False or defamatory allegations are grounds for immediate removal and may have legal consequences.

  • 🚫

    Personal identifying information

    Do not include full names, phone numbers, email addresses, financial information, ID numbers, or other personal details of individuals in your review.

  • 🚫

    Promotional or spam content

    Reviews are for genuine rental experiences only. Promotional content, advertisements, links, or unrelated content will be removed.

  • 🚫

    Conflict of interest reviews

    Property owners, agents, or anyone with a financial interest in a property may not submit reviews for that property. This includes reviews intended to artificially improve ratings.

Moderation and enforcement

All reviews are manually reviewed by our moderation team before publication. We do not auto-publish any content. During review, we assess submissions against these guidelines and may:

  • Approve and publish the review as submitted
  • Request clarification or edits from the submitter
  • Decline to publish without detailed explanation
  • Remove a published review if a valid complaint is received

Homredy's decision on moderation is final. If you believe a review violates these guidelines, please contact us at moderation@homredy.com.
